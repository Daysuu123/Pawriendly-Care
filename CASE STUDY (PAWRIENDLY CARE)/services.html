Our Services

"Where Every Paw Receives Loving Care"

Comprehensive veterinary services for your beloved pets

Complete Pet Care Solutions

From preventive care to emergency services, we've got you covered

Preventive Care

Prevention is the key to your pet's health. Our wellness programs start with a comprehensive physical exam and includes a vaccination program, internal and external parasite control and prevention, and advice on general pet care and nutrition for all life stages.

  • Comprehensive physical examinations
  • Vaccination programs
  • Parasite control & prevention
  • Nutritional counseling
  • Dental care

Emergency Care

We provide 24/7 emergency care services to handle any urgent medical needs your pet may have. If your pet is experiencing a life-threatening emergency and needs to be seen immediately. Our veterinarian on duty can assess the nature of the emergency and give you advice while you are on your way.

  • 24/7 emergency services
  • Critical care monitoring
  • Emergency surgery
  • Trauma care
  • Poisoning treatment

Home Visits

Our home visit services allow your pet to receive veterinary care in the comfort of your home. In general, we recommend you visit a comprehensively equipped and staffed practice near you if your pet is ill. However, we do understand there are occasions where this might not be possible.

  • In-home consultations
  • Vaccinations at home
  • Senior pet check-ups
  • End-of-life care
  • Stress-free environment

Surgery

Our teams of veterinary surgeons perform routine elective procedures like spaying and neutering, as well as complicated soft-tissue and orthopedic surgeries, following the strictest surgical guidelines to minimize risks and complications.

  • Spaying & neutering
  • Soft tissue surgery
  • Orthopedic surgery
  • Dental surgery
  • Advanced monitoring

Grooming

We offer comprehensive grooming packages for both dogs and cats, as well as short- and long-term accommodation packages for owners who need to leave their pets in our care temporarily. We also have in-house pet stores.

  • Bathing & brushing
  • Haircuts & styling
  • Nail trimming
  • Ear cleaning
  • Pet boarding services

Therapy & Rehabilitation

We have different programs to increase mobility, reduce pain and promote healing from surgery, injury or chronic conditions. Should clients prefer non-invasive, drug-free treatments for their pets.

  • Laser therapy
  • Ultrasound therapy
  • Hydrotherapy
  • Electrostimulation
  • Physical rehabilitation
